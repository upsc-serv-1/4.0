# UPSC GS Mains Syllabus Hierarchy (GS-I to GS-IV)

This merged document outlines the structured taxonomy of all four General Studies papers (GS-I, GS-II, GS-III, and GS-IV) for the UPSC Civil Services Mains Examination.

---
