###### Card 1: Comparison with Constitutions of Other Countries
*   **Quote:**
    > **"While we borrowed from many, we built for Bharat." — Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**
*   **Introduction:**
    *   A unique blend of global constitutional wisdom, **India’s constitutional design marries UK’s parliamentary ethics, US’s checks-and-balances, and Irish DPSPs to craft a Bharatiya model.**
*   **Examples:**
    *   Law Commission proposed selective adoption of AI laws from EU
    *   SC cited US Supreme Court precedents in digital privacy verdict
    *   NITI Aayog referenced OECD models for cooperative federalism
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   Learning globally but acting locally keeps Indian constitutionalism adaptive and culturally rooted.
###### Card 2: PARLIAMENTARY DEMOCRACY vs PRESIDENTIAL SYSTEM
*   **Quote:**
    > **"Parliamentary democracy is the bedrock of India's governance, where diverse voices converge to shape the nation's destiny." — ABV (Atal Bihari Vajpayee)**
*   **Introduction:**
    *   **Parliamentary System:** Form of democracy where the **executive branch is accountable to the legislature**, with a **PM as head of government**. Part of **Basic Structure**.
    *   **Presidential System:** Form of democracy where the **executive is separate from the legislature**, with a **directly elected President** as both head of state and government.

###### Card 1: FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS (Frs)
*   **Introduction:**
    *   Begin with **specific article** reference.
    *   **Fundamental Rights** are the basic human rights enshrined in the Constitution (**Part 3 — Art. 12–35**), guaranteed to **all citizens**.
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   **FRs create conditions** for **moral and material progress** of citizens while ensuring **constitutionalism/limited govt** in line with "We the People."
###### Card 2: PREAMBLE
*   **Quote:**
    > **"Horoscope of our sovereign democratic republic." — KM Munshi**
*   **Introduction:**
    *   **Introductory statement** in the Constitution that explains its **philosophy and objectives**.
    *   **LIC case & Kesavananda Bharati** — Part of the Constitution but **not directly enforceable**.
###### Card 3: RESERVATION / AFFIRMATIVE ACTION
*   **Quote:**
    > **"Purpose of reservation has been stretched beyond combating discrimination & empowering marginalised to an anti-poverty measure — a load it cannot bear." — Pratap Bhanu Mehta**
    > **"Strong consensus for weak reforms." — Montek Singh Ahluwalia**
*   **Introduction:**
    *   **Affirmative action** taken by the State to remove **persistent discrimination** on a particular segment of society.
    *   **Context:** Haryana local job quota.
    *   **Key Judgements:**
        *   **Nagraj Case:** (1) Equality is part of Basic Structure; (2) **Width & Identity Test** — Width: 50% limit, creamy layer, compelling reasons, administrative efficiency; Identity: BSD.
        *   **Indira Sawhney Case:** Struck down **10% reservation** by Narsimha Rao for General Category.
        *   **NALSA, Ram Singh vs UOI:** State is duty-bound to discover new emerging groups.
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   **Justice Ranganath Mishra Commission:** Bring **uniform criteria** based on **socio-economic backwardness**.
###### Card 4: FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES (Fds)
*   **Quote:**
    > **"The true source of rights is duty." — Gandhi**
*   **Introduction:**
    *   **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act (CAA) 1976** added **Part 4A** on recommendations of the **Swaran Singh Committee**, providing for **moral obligations** of citizens towards the state and fellow citizens.
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   Promises of **Part 3 (FRs) & Part 4 (DPSPs)** cannot be fulfilled without effective realization of citizen duties under **Part 4A**.
###### Card 5: SEDITION (Section 124a IPC)
*   **Quote:**
    > **"124A is the prince among sections of IPC to suppress liberty of people." — Gandhiji**
*   **Key Judgements:**
        *   **Kedarnath vs State of Bihar, Arup Bhuyan Case:** Sedition applies **only if incitement to violence**.
        *   **SG Vombatkere vs UOI:** SC suspended operation of 124A pending review; colonial legacy.
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   **Kishan Bhai vs State of Gujarat:** Policemen must be prosecuted for filing chargesheet against a person knowing he was innocent.
    *   **"Cutting the tree vs cutting the entire forest"** — must be proportionate.
    *   **"Process becomes the punishment"** — danger of misuse.
*   **Introduction:**
    *   **Context:** Law Commission's **279th Report** recently reviewed and recommended retaining **S. 124A**.
###### Card 6: FREEDOM of SPEECH & EXPRESSION (FOSE) / DISSENT
*   **Quote:**
    > **"Dissent is the Safety Valve of democracy." — DY Chandrachud**
    > **"Main challenge to FOSE is not state-imposed restrictions but demands of masses who favor limitations of speech — Heckler's veto." — Abhinav Chandrachud, *Republic of Rhetoric***
    > **"Censorial powers is in the people over govt and not in govt over people."**
*   **Key Judgements:**
    *   **FOSE rests on 2 premises:**
        *   **Instrumental value theory** — means to an end (democracy, truth, art)
        *   **Intrinsic value theory** — liberty, self-development to full potential
###### Card 7: INTERNET SHUTDOWN
*   **Introduction:**
    *   Govt measures to **prevent/disrupt access to the internet**.
    *   **Context:** Manipur internet shutdown.
    *   **Anuradha Bhasin Case:** Indefinite suspension of internet services contravenes **Art. 19(1)(a) & 19(1)(g)**.
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   Parliamentary Standing Committee recommendations must be followed — **clear policies & selective, targeted restrictions**.
*   **Data:**
    *   India topped global list of documented internet shutdowns with **116 disruptions in 2023 (Access Now)**.
    *   **Brookings Study 2016:** Economic loss of **$970 million** in 2016.
###### Card 8: DEFAMATION
*   **Introduction:**
    *   **Context:** An MP sentenced to 2 years (criminal defamation) u/s **499, 500 IPC** by Surat HC.
    *   **One of the Reasonable Restrictions (RR)** under **Art. 19(2)**.
    *   Act of communicating **false statements** about a person that injure their reputation.
    *   **Subramanyam Swamy Case:** Upheld — right to reputation under **Art. 21** and need for constitutional fraternity.
    *   Punishment > 2 years → ground for **disqualification** under **S. 8(3) of RPA**.
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   **Freedom comes with responsibility.** Being an enumerated RR — need to interpret each case with utmost care to **balance Art. 19 with right to reputation under Art. 21**.
###### Card 9: UNIFORM CIVIL CODE (UCC)
*   **Quote:**
    > **"UCC should apply only for those who are prepared to be bound by it." — Ambedkar**
*   **Introduction:**
    *   **Art. 44** — Single law for personal matters based on the presumption that **no connection exists between religion & law** in modern civilization.
    *   **Pannalal Patil Case:** Despite desirability of UCC, its enactment may be **counterproductive to unity**; bottom-up force required.
    *   **Law Commission:** Mere difference is not discrimination; rather indicates a robust democracy.
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   **Piecemeal** rather than **wholesale social engineering**.
    *   **Law Commission:** In absence of consensus in a diverse country, best way is to **preserve diversity** and ensure personal laws don't contradict FRs through **amendment of inequalities**.

###### Card 11: DPDP Bill (Data Protection)
*   **Introduction:**
    *   Recently DPDP Bill was passed with obj of processing **digital personal data** while balancing **privacy** & need to process data for lawful purposes.
    *   Data is the **new oil**.
    *   **Big data - 4Vs**: Veracity, Volume, Variety, Velocity.
