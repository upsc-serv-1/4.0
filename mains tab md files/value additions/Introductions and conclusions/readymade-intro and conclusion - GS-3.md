###### Card 1: Ease of Doing Business & PLI
*   **Quote:**
    > **"From red tape to red carpet is a journey of mindset and law."**
*   **Introduction:**
    *   Improving the **ease of doing business** through reforms like the **Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code**, **PLI schemes**, and **faceless approvals** reflects the shift to a **“compliance-light, trust-heavy” economy.**
*   **Examples:**
    *   IBC enabled recovery of **₹2.9 lakh crore** in major insolvency cases.
    *   **PLI schemes across 14 sectors** attracted investment and scaled exports.
    *   MCA21, GSTN, and single-window clearances boosted **regulatory transparency**.
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   India must deepen reforms in **contract enforcement and informal sector inclusion** for holistic ease.
###### Card 2: FDI/FII Trends & Trade Policies
*   **Quote:**
    > **"Capital follows clarity, consistency, and confidence."**
*   **Introduction:**
    *   India’s evolving **FDI and trade policies**, coupled with improved ease of doing business, aim to harness **“trust reshoring”** and make India a **key node in global value chains.**
*   **Examples:**
    *   India received **$80+ billion FDI**, led by renewables, electronics, and services.
    *   Sectoral caps liberalised in **insurance (74%) and defence (74% automatic, 100% via govt route)**; **100% FDI permitted in space sector** under amended rules.
    *   India signed **FTA pacts with UAE and Australia** for export market access.
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   A balance of **open markets and strategic safeguards** is vital for resilient industrial expansion.
