# UPSC GS Mains Paper 4 (GS-IV) - Ethics Complete Quotes & Philosophical Compilation (2013-2025)

This document compiles all the philosophical, moral, and administrative quotations used by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) in the GS-IV (Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude) Mains examinations. These are structured into two sections: **Core Thinker & Philosopher Quotations** (interpreting quotes by major ethical leaders) and **Key Administrative Maxims & Conceptual Statements**.

> [!TIP]
> **How to Answer Quotation-Based Questions (10 Marks, 150 Words):**
> 1. **Deconstruct the Quote (30-40 words)**: Explain the quote's core meaning in simple terms. Mention the thinker and their philosophical school (e.g., Kantian Deontology, Gandhian Trusteeship, Virtue Ethics).
> 2. **Administrative Application (80-90 words)**: Connect the value to public service. How does this help a civil servant handle ethical dilemmas, conflicts of interest, or decision-making?
> 3. **Substantiate with Examples**: Provide concrete examples of legendary public servants (e.g., Lal Bahadur Shastri, E. Sreedharan), historical figures, or contemporary situations.
> 4. **Conclusion (20-30 words)**: End with a forward-looking thought on how this principle ensures ethical governance and public welfare.
