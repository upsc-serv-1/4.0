# Khemka Sir's Ethical Rules for Decision Making
---

### Rule 1: The Difficult-Choice Rule
*   **Concept:** Of two or three options, the harder one is almost always ethical; the easy, convenient (*suvidhavadi*) option usually slides toward corruption. Ethical conduct is measured by the benefit you forgo and the hardship you accept.
*   **Metadata Tags:**
    *   **Core Value:** `Integrity`, `Ethical Fortitude`
    *   **Syllabus Subtopic:** `Conscience as Source of Guidance`, `Ethical Dilemmas`
    *   **Situation Type:** `Hard Choices`, `Personal vs Professional Conflict`
    *   **Action Directive:** `Forgoing Self-Interest`

---

### Rule 2: The Sunshine Test
*   **Concept:** Do only what you could state openly, in public, and still feel proud of. If you wouldn't say it in the open, don't do it.
*   **Metadata Tags:**
    *   **Core Value:** `Transparency`, `Openness`
    *   **Syllabus Subtopic:** `Information Sharing and Transparency`
    *   **Situation Type:** `Discretionary Powers`, `Public Scrutiny`
    *   **Action Directive:** `Public Disclosure`

---

### Rule 3: Public Interest is Supreme
*   **Concept:** A public servant is paid by the public and is its servant; public interest can never be sacrificed for private interest. Working against public interest is itself corruption, even with no bribe.
*   **Metadata Tags:**
    *   **Core Value:** `Dedication to Public Service`, `Responsibility`
    *   **Syllabus Subtopic:** `Concept of Public Service`, `Philosophical Basis of Governance`
    *   **Situation Type:** `Conflict of Interest`, `Policy Implementation`
    *   **Action Directive:** `Prioritizing Public Good`

---

### Rule 4: No 'Big' vs 'Small' Corruption
*   **Concept:** Corruption is corruption; 'petty corruption oils the engine' is a rationalisation. But don't be a rigid rule-bound 'lakeer ka fakeer' - judge by your larger intent.
*   **Metadata Tags:**
    *   **Core Value:** `Zero Tolerance`, `Moral Clarity`
    *   **Syllabus Subtopic:** `Challenges of Corruption`
    *   **Situation Type:** `Systemic Pressure`, `Routine Bribery`
    *   **Action Directive:** `Zero Tolerance Policy`

---

### Rule 5: Without Fear or Favour
*   **Concept:** The Constitution requires it. Paying a goonda tax out of fear, or bending a decision for a favour, both fail this test.
*   **Metadata Tags:**
    *   **Core Value:** `Impartiality`, `Non-Partisanship`, `Courage`
    *   **Syllabus Subtopic:** `Foundational Values for Civil Service`
    *   **Situation Type:** `Political Pressure`, `Security Threat`, `Favoritism`
    *   **Action Directive:** `Constitutional Adherence`

---

### Rule 6: Recusal Justice Must be Seen to be Done
*   **Concept:** Where a relative or personal interest is involved, step away; disclose the conflict. In a private firm, disclose to top management and act only if they still entrust you.
*   **Metadata Tags:**
    *   **Core Value:** `Objectivity`, `Fairness`
    *   **Syllabus Subtopic:** `Ethical Concerns and Dilemmas`
    *   **Situation Type:** `Conflict of Interest`, `Nepotism`, `Tendering & Procurement`
    *   **Action Directive:** `Recusal & Disclosure`

---

### Rule 7: Whistleblowing Belongs to the Private Sector
*   **Concept:** It is loyalty to the company vs the larger loyalty to the public. In the public sector, the same act is simply duty, because a public servant already owes loyalty only to the public.
*   **Metadata Tags:**
    *   **Core Value:** `Accountability`, `Public Trust`
    *   **Syllabus Subtopic:** `Codes of Ethics and Conduct`
    *   **Situation Type:** `Exposing Wrongdoing`, `Corporate Misconduct`
    *   **Action Directive:** `Reporting & Duty`

---

### Rule 8: Resistance Reduces Injustice
*   **Concept:** Against entrenched wrongdoing, surrender lets it grow; resistance shrinks the wrongdoer's power. Stay optimistic that truth prevails.
*   **Metadata Tags:**
    *   **Core Value:** `Fortitude`, `Optimism`
    *   **Syllabus Subtopic:** `Strengthening of Ethical Values`
    *   **Situation Type:** `Systemic Injustice`, `Entrenched Malpractice`
    *   **Action Directive:** `Active Resistance`

---

### Rule 9: Dignity is Non-Negotiable
*   **Concept:** An individual's dignity, especially of women at the workplace, cannot be exchanged for profit; a high-performing harasser still faces action.
*   **Metadata Tags:**
    *   **Core Value:** `Human Dignity`, `Gender Justice`
    *   **Syllabus Subtopic:** `Ethics in Human Interface`, `Work Culture`
    *   **Situation Type:** `Workplace Harassment`, `POSH Act Compliance`
    *   **Action Directive:** `Zero Tolerance for Harassment`

---

### Rule 10: Speed and Fairness in Process
*   **Concept:** 'Justice delayed is justice denied' but 'justice hurried is justice buried' too. False complaints should carry the same punishment as the offence they falsely allege.
*   **Metadata Tags:**
    *   **Core Value:** `Justice`, `Due Process`, `Fairness`
    *   **Syllabus Subtopic:** `Quality of Service Delivery`
    *   **Situation Type:** `Enquiry Process`, `Administrative Action`
    *   **Action Directive:** `Balanced Investigation`
