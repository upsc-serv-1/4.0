Q1. What are the characteristics of ideal health indicators?
(a) They should be valid, reliable, sensitive, specific, feasible and relevant
(b) They should be mainly valid, reliable and sensitive but need not be specific

Q3. The yield of a screening test CAN NOT be increased by which of the following?
(a) Including entire population
(b) Including high risk population
(c) Improved sensitivity
(d) Improved specificity

3. Solution (a)
Explanation: Yield of screening is the number of new cases detected. It is increased by screening high-risk populations (higher prevalence), improving sensitivity (detects more true cases), and improving specificity (reduces false positives but does not increase yield directly). Screening the entire population (low-risk) does not increase yield per unit cost; it may increase false positives. Thus (a) cannot increase yield.
