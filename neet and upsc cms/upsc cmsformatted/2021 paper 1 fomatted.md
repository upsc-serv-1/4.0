Q1. A 60-year-old man comes to emergency with history of chest pain, which is acute onset. The ECG shows ST depression and T waves inversion. Cardiac biomarkers in blood are not elevated. What will be the appropriate management?
(a) Oxygen + Aspirin + Ticagrelor + Metoprolol
(b) Oxygen + Sublingual nitroglycerin + Antiplatelets + Streptokinase
(c) Antiplatelets + LMW Heparin subcutaneous
(d) Oxygen + Sublingual nitroglycerin + Digoxin

1. Solution (c)
Explanation: The patient has non-ST elevation acute coronary syndrome (NSTE-ACS) with negative biomarkers (unstable angina). Appropriate management includes antiplatelets (aspirin + P2Y12 inhibitor) and anticoagulation (LMW heparin). Streptokinase is not indicated for NSTE-ACS. Digoxin is not first-line.

Q120. Which one of the following diseases is covered under Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK)?
(a) Rheumatic heart disease
(b) Community acquired pneumonia
(c) Systemic onset juvenile idiopathic arthritis
(d) Nephrotic syndrome

120. Solution (a)
Explanation: RBSK (Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram) screens children from birth to 18 years for 4 Ds (Defects, Diseases, Deficiencies, Developmental delays). Rheumatic heart disease (RHD) is a screened condition under the program. Community acquired pneumonia, JIA, and nephrotic syndrome are not specifically listed as screening targets under RBSK's 30 conditions.
