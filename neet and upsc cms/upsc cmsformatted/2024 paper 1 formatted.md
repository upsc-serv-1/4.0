Q4. Reverse splitting of second heart sound is most commonly seen in
(a) ASD
(b) Severe MR
(c) LBBB
(d) Pulmonary hypertension

4. Solution (c)
Explanation: Reverse (paradoxical) splitting of S2 means the pulmonic component (P2) occurs before the aortic component (A2) during expiration, and splitting narrows or disappears on inspiration. It is most commonly associated with left bundle branch block (LBBB), which delays aortic valve closure. Other causes include severe aortic stenosis and hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.

(a) it might compromise uptake of vaccine
(b) it could trigger an episode of intussusception
(c) Rotavirus vaccine is a live vaccine and hence may cause rotavirus diarrhoea and worsen the diarrheal episode
(d) vomiting is a significant adverse effect of Rotavirus vaccine

120. Solution (c)
Explanation: Rotavirus vaccine is a live oral vaccine. If given during diarrheal illness, it may be poorly absorbed or cause vaccine-strain rotavirus diarrhea, worsening the episode. Thus (c) is correct.
