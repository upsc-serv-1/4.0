Q1. Vibration white finger refers to :
(a) Buerger's disease
(b) Raynaud's syndrome
(c) Acrocyanosis
(d) Takayasu disease

1. Solution (b)
Explanation: Vibration white finger is a form of secondary Raynaud's phenomenon caused by repeated use of vibrating tools, leading to vasospasm of digital arteries. II. Colour blindness
III. Cystic fibrosis
IV. Duchenne muscular dystrophy
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
(a) I, II and III
(b) I, II and IV
(c) II, III and IV
(d) I, III and IV

119. Solution (b)
Explanation: Sex-linked recessive disorders include retinitis pigmentosa (some forms), colour blindness (red-green), and Duchenne muscular dystrophy. Cystic fibrosis is autosomal recessive. Hence I, II, IV correct.
