THE DISCIPLINE, DEFINED

What Is Sociology?

Sociology is the systematic study of society — of social relationships, institutions, and the shared patterns that hold group life together. It asks why people, groups and institutions behave the way they do.
WHAT WE STUDY
Social Structure & Institutions
Family, marriage, religion, economy and polity as interlinked systems.
Stratification & Social Change
Class, caste, gender and the forces that drive or resist change.
Culture & Socialization
How norms, values and identity are learned, shared and passed on.
Sociological Theory
Thinkers and perspectives used to explain and interpret social life.
